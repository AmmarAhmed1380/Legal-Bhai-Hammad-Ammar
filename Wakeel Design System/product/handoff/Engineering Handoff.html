01 Context · what we are shipping

Wakeel v1 is a three-surface SaaS — a lawyer's mobile app, a firm dashboard, and a client portal — sharing one backend. The v1 scope is Islamabad-first: live cause-list sync from IHC + Islamabad District + Rawalpindi District. All other courts are seeded demo data, clearly labelled in the UI.

06 Client portal · 5 screens

Public, tokenized, no-install. Each client gets one long-lived URL (wakeel.pk/c/[token]) that lists all their matters with that firm. Existing /share/[token] becomes a 301 redirect.

SMS-deliverability note: The token in the URL MUST fit a 160-char SMS budget after the Urdu/English body, firm name, and next-hearing line. Keep tokens to 22 chars max (base62 × 22 ≈ 130 bits of entropy — plenty).

10 Court ingestion · the actual moat

Each court is a BullMQ job ladder: fetch → parse → normalise → match → diff → notify. The matching step is where we attach cause-list entries to existing firm cases by case number or party-name fuzzy match (p≥0.92).

CourtSourceIntervalParseSLO
IHCStructured HTML (causelist.ihc.gov.pk)15 min, court hrsCheerio selectors99.4% accuracy
LHCJSON API · 4 benches15 minDirect99.1%
Supreme CourtStructured HTML30 minCheerio99.7%
Islamabad DistrictRoster JSON feedHourlyDirect97.8%
Rawalpindi DistrictRoster JSON feedHourlyDirect97.2%
SHC / PHC / BHCPDF · dailyDailypdfplumber → gpt-4o reviewpilot — human in the loop
Reliability budget: ingestion downtime is MORE visible than app downtime. If IHC is missed for a single day, three lawyer-hours of trust evaporate. SLO = 99.5% on-time runs per month; breach triggers on-call in the Islamabad ops team, not engineering.

12 i18n & RTL — Urdu is a first-class locale

English is the default at launch; Urdu ships in the same binary, toggleable per-user. Sindhi + Punjabi are post-v1.

  • i18next on both apps with namespaces: common, mobile, dashboard, portal, marketing, ingestion (for SMS templates).
  • Noto Nastaliq Urdu for all Urdu copy; keep numerals in Western Arabic (1234) because PK legal users read case numbers that way.
  • RTL only flips layout for Urdu — English+Urdu bilingual strings (common on SMS) stay LTR in composition mode.
  • Date formats localise via date-fns-tz + Pakistan-specific format (d MMM, always 24-hour). Court-day names in Urdu come from a static map, not ICU.
  • SMS templates are Urdu-default — most clients read them on feature phones. See apps/api/src/sms/templates/.

14 Open questions · decisions we still owe

Blocker — push notifications on budget Androids. Firebase works but Jazz-SIM users on MTN / Zong with aggressive battery savers miss ~18% of alerts. Evaluating: (a) Jazz direct-SMS fallback for high-priority alerts only; (b) self-hosted MQTT with WebSocket polling. Owner: Bilal. Due W4.
  • E2E chat key management — sticking with Signal-protocol shim or rolling simpler double-ratchet internally? Asma wants bar-council-auditable keys.
  • Cross-firm conflict-check — the deepest version needs a global case-party index. Do we honour "opt-in anonymous hashing" (each firm contributes hashed party names) or keep conflict-check firm-local in v1?
  • PDF viewer in portal — PDF.js is heavy. Do we pre-render to WebP pages on upload and show those by default? Decision needed before W8.
  • Partner-track firms can A/B test. Which firms pilot which features? Ops to propose by W2.
  • Marketing DNS — wakeel.pk or wakeel.com.pk as primary? Registrar preference sits with finance.
